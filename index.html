Privacy Policy — ABG Master Interpreter

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Publisher: Medicine Beyond Harrison

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ABG Master Interpreter, an Android application intended to support arterial blood gas (ABG) interpretation and clinical learning, handles information. The application is intended for healthcare professionals and learners. Please read this policy before using the application.

2. Information entered into the application

The application may allow users to enter clinical measurements, interpretation inputs, notes, or patient-record details. These entries are designed to be stored locally on the device using the application's local storage mechanisms. The developer does not operate a server to collect these clinical entries. The application is not intended to transmit locally stored patient records to the developer.

Local storage is not a substitute for an approved hospital record system. Anyone using the application must avoid entering identifiable patient information unless authorised to do so and consistent with applicable institutional policies and law.

3. Offline functionality and internet access

Core ABG calculation and interpretation features are designed to work offline. However, certain optional or integrated services, including advertisements, consent management, and Google Play purchase verification, may require an internet connection. Accordingly, the application should not be described as entirely offline in every feature or circumstance.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The application integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interaction, diagnostic information, approximate location inferred from IP address, and advertising-related data, depending on the device, settings, consent choices, and applicable Google services.

Google's handling of information is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and publisher policies

The application also integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable advertising consent and privacy choices. Consent options and the availability of personalised advertising depend on the user's location, applicable requirements, and consent configuration.

5. In-app purchases

The application may offer a one-time purchase to remove advertisements. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. The developer does not receive or store the user's full payment card details through the application. Google Play handles payment processing and purchase records under its own terms and privacy policies.

For information, see Google Payments and the Google Privacy Policy .

6. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell locally stored clinical entries or patient-record details. The developer does not operate a remote clinical-record collection service through this application. Third-party service providers, including Google, may independently process information as described in this policy and their own documentation to provide advertising, consent, security, and billing services.

7. Data retention and deletion

Clinical entries stored locally remain on the device until deleted through available application functions or removed through relevant device or application storage actions. Clearing application data or uninstalling the application may permanently remove locally stored records. Users should export or otherwise securely preserve any information they need before clearing data or uninstalling.

The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete records that exist only in local application storage. Information processed by Google services is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices.

8. Children's privacy

This application is intended for healthcare professionals, medical students, and other appropriate adult users. It is not designed specifically for children. The publisher does not knowingly use the application to collect personal information directly from children.

9. Security

Users should secure their devices, use appropriate screen locks, avoid entering unnecessary identifiable patient information, and follow their institution's data-protection policies. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Medical disclaimer

This application is an educational and clinical-support tool. It is not a substitute for clinical judgement, patient assessment, local protocols, or specialist consultation. Users are responsible for verifying calculations and interpretations and making clinical decisions using the complete clinical context.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date shown at the top will be revised when appropriate. Users should review this page periodically.
